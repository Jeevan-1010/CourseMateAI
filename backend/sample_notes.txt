Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that develops systems capable of tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI. It allows computers to learn patterns from data rather than relying only on explicitly programmed rules.

There are three main types of machine learning:
1. Supervised learning uses labeled training data.
2. Unsupervised learning discovers patterns in data without labels.
3. Reinforcement learning learns through rewards and penalties.

A neural network consists of interconnected neurons arranged in input, hidden, and output layers.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines document retrieval with a language model. Its six main stages are Load, Split, Embed, Store, Retrieve, and Generate.

RAG helps students ask questions about their study materials and receive answers grounded in the information retrieved from their documents.
